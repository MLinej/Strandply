How this is worked out

Good boards = boards − rejects on submitted and approved shifts; voided entries excluded. Sqm uses each SKU’s size.
Material = quantity consumed × weighted average purchase cost (ex GST, per kg / pcs / sqm) over the selected period.
Power = main meter readings × tariff, shown beside the PGVCL bill where one is entered. A month is flagged when any input is missing.
